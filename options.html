QueueDeck settings

Everything below is stored only on this device. QueueDeck has no server and no account.

Prompt me while I'm watching

Show a small "Save video?" prompt after you've been watching a video for a while.

Applies on any site with a video, from 5 seconds up to 3 minutes.

Keyboard shortcut

Press Ctrl+Shift+S (⌃+⇧+S on Mac) on any tab to save its video straight to QueueDeck — no need to open the popup. The toolbar icon flashes ✓ to confirm (or · if it was already saved).

To change it, go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts and find QueueDeck in the list.

Your data

QueueDeck collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. Your saved list lives in this browser's local storage and survives restarts and extension updates — it only leaves this device if you export it yourself.

QueueDeck is free and open source.
