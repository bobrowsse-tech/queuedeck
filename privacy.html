QueueDeck

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

QueueDeck is a Chrome extension that saves video links into named lists on this device. This policy explains what the extension does — and does not — do with your data.

Summary

  • Everything stays on your device.
  • No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The extension never sends your data over the network.

What we store

QueueDeck stores your playlist and settings only in Chrome’s local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your computer. That may include:

  • Video URL, title, thumbnail URL, and site name
  • Optional notes you write
  • Watched state and playback resume position
  • Settings such as prompt delay, theme, and sort/filter preferences
  • Which list is open, and which list saves from a page go to
  • List names, and whether a list is locked

This data is cleared only when you clear it in the Options page, clear site/extension data in Chrome, or uninstall the extension.

A list lock is a screen gate. QueueDeck stores only a credential id for the check. It never stores a fingerprint, password, or PIN. The saved videos are not encrypted. After you unlock a list, that permission lives in chrome.storage.session and ends when the browser session ends. Saving into a locked list still works.

What we do not collect

  • No personal identity, email, or account information
  • No browsing history beyond the pages where you choose to save a video
  • No analytics, crash reports, advertising IDs, or usage telemetry
  • No remote logging or “phone home” requests of any kind

Permissions

  • storage — save your playlist and settings locally.
  • activeTab — read the current tab’s URL/title only when you open the popup or use the save shortcut.
  • Content script on all pages — detect <video> elements and page metadata (title, Open Graph image/site name) so the save prompt can work on any video site. It does not read form fields, passwords, payment data, or unrelated page content, and it does not transmit anything.

Network activity

QueueDeck itself initiates zero network requests. Thumbnails may load from the original video site when Chrome displays an image URL you already saved; that is standard browser image loading, not an extension analytics channel.

Children

QueueDeck is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes
